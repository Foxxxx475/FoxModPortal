ABOUT

Built as a clean catalog, not a landing page.

The public site is meant to be a readable mod catalog with a straightforward submission flow and a visual style that stays out of the way.

Purpose

List community mods, explain the files, and keep the publishing flow simple.

Design rules

Flat background, one accent color, left-aligned text, and no animated decoration for its own sake.
