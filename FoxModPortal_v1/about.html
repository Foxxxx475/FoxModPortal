ABOUT

Built around the community.

Fox Mod Portal starts with Tactical Assault VR and is designed to grow carefully from there.

Mission

Make discovering and sharing standalone VR mods straightforward without turning a mod library into an account-first social network.

Principles

  • Privacy by default.
  • No forced accounts for basic participation.
  • Clear mod metadata and compatibility information.
  • Creators control what information they publish.
  • Transparent separation between the portal and game runtime.

Roadmap

v1.0Public catalog

Browse, search, docs, local submissions.

v1.1Anonymous publishing backend

Optional upload flow without required user accounts.

v2.0Quest companion

Native headset workflow and mod management.

FutureTAVR runtime integration

Only once a stable, legitimate loading path is established.
