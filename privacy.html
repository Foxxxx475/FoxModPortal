Privacy by default.

Fox Mod Portal v1 is intentionally simple about what it asks from visitors.

What v1 does not require

  • No user account.
  • No email address for the submission form.
  • No profile information.
  • No built-in advertising or analytics scripts.

Local submission behavior

The submit page creates JSON and Markdown from the information you enter. Generation, copying, and downloading happen in your browser. The v1 page does not send the form contents to a Fox Mod Portal server.

Hosting caveat

The hosting provider, DNS provider, repository provider, or network may still keep ordinary technical logs. Fox Mod Portal does not control those systems, so the site does not promise that visiting a hosted website leaves no technical logs anywhere.
