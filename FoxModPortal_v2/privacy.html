PRIVACY

Privacy-first by default.

The v2 site keeps the public flow simple and does not require a login for browsing or preparing a submission packet.

What the site does not require

  • No account for browsing.
  • No email for the form.
  • No built-in analytics scripts.
  • No automatic uploads from the submission page.

Local submission behavior

The submit page turns form data into JSON and Markdown in the browser. You decide where to send it.

Hosting note

The hosting provider and network may still keep technical logs. This page describes the site’s own behavior, not the behavior of every service around it.
