# Privacy policy

Fox Mod Portal v1 does not require user accounts and does not include built-in advertising or analytics scripts.

The submission page processes form fields locally in the visitor's browser and does not automatically upload them.

Third-party infrastructure can still maintain ordinary technical logs. Those are controlled by hosting, network, DNS, or repository providers.

This policy describes the behavior of the v1 static site and should be updated if the project later adds server-side publishing, authentication, comments, ratings, or analytics.
