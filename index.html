FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this require an account?

No. The submission form generates a file locally and keeps the process private.

Does the site upload anything automatically?

No. The browser only creates a downloadable packet. Nothing leaves the device by default.

Can this become a real workshop later?

Yes. A backend can be added later for automatic publishing, ratings, and comments.
